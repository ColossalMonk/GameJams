Pop Squad

Tap a critter to pop it! Same-colored friends nearby — even diagonally — chain right along. Watch for sparkly power-up critters, and chase the biggest cascade before the grid fills up or time runs out.



🐸

How to Play

🟡 Tap any critter to pop it.

🔗 Same-colored critters touching it — including diagonally — pop too, and so on down the chain.

📏 Bigger critters are worth more points, and any size can chain into any other size, as long as the color matches.

✨ Rainbow critters pop every neighbor no matter the color.
💣 Bomb critters clear everything around them.
⭐ Star critters give bonus points and a few extra seconds.

⏳ New critters keep spawning. If the grid fills up completely, it's game over — so keep popping!
