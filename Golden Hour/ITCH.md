# itch.io release kit — Golden Hour

Everything needed to publish Golden Hour on itch.io for the [20 Second Game Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/20-second-game-jam-2026). Submissions close **Nov 16, 2026**.

## 1. Build and package

From the `Golden Hour` folder, in PowerShell:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\package-itch.ps1
```

This builds the game and writes `golden-hour-itch.zip`, with `index.html` at the root of the zip.

Don't use `Compress-Archive` for this. In Windows PowerShell 5.1 it writes paths like `assets\file.js` with a backslash, which itch.io can treat as part of the file name, and the game then fails to load.

## 2. Create the project page

- **Kind of project:** HTML
- **Upload:** `golden-hour-itch.zip`, ticked "This file will be played in the browser"
- **Embed options:**
  - Viewport size 960 × 540
  - Tick "Mobile friendly" (orientation: landscape)
  - Tick "Fullscreen button"
- **Classification:** Games · Genre: Simulation / Puzzle · Tags: `20-second-game-jam`, `photography`, `cozy`, `short`, `paper`, `three.js`
- **AI generation disclosure:** set the field to **Yes, this project includes generative AI output**. Then add the line from section 4 to the description. The jam rules require this.
- **Submit to the jam** from the jam page once the project is published (or public with the jam link).

## 3. Page text (paste and edit)

> **Golden Hour**
>
> The sun is swelling and the world has twenty seconds left. Nobody minds. The town is out for one last perfect evening, and you're the photographer with five shots on the roll.
>
> Every evening is different. Tonight's stories are picked from more than fifty: a dog leaping for a frisbee, an old car that finally starts, a piano on a crane, a bride's bouquet, a marching band, sky lanterns. Any of them can go the other way. Things thrown in one story land in another. And on some evenings something rare happens. Or something legendary.
>
> Frame it, catch it at its peak, and see your evening come back as an album of polaroids.
>
> **Controls**
> - Mouse / touch: move to aim (drag on touch)
> - Scroll / pinch, or Q and E: zoom
> - Click / tap / Space: take a photo (you have five)
> - Arrow keys or WASD: aim with the keyboard
>
> **Tips:** a chime and a little sparkle mean something is about to happen. Tighter framing and later, golder light score more. Hold still when you shoot. Every evening has a number: "Today's evening" is the same for everyone, so compare albums with friends.
>
> Made for the 20 Second Game Jam 2026.

## 5. Before publishing: a quick checklist

- [ ] Play 3–4 evenings start to finish in the built version (`node node_modules/vite/bin/vite.js preview`)
- [ ] Sound plays after the first click (browsers block audio until then)
- [ ] Try it on a phone in landscape: aim, pinch, tap, album scrolls
- [ ] "Save album" downloads a PNG; "Copy result" copies a line (inside the itch frame it may fall back to a copy prompt)
- [ ] The cover image (630 × 500) and 3–5 screenshots are uploaded. Good subjects: the train crossing the sun, an album page, a crossover (the hat landing on someone)
